A new look for cards and popovers, and drag-and-drop between the calendar and the sidebar.

- **One card for tasks in the same slot.** Tasks booked into the same time show as a single card with a count and a segmented bar. Click it to see them side by side, reorder them, or drag one out onto its own time.
- **Liquid Glass popovers.** Every popover is now the same rounded glass card, without the pointer tip.
- **Drag a sidebar project onto the calendar** to block out time for it. The new block shows up right away, in Today if it's booked for today.
- **Drag a block onto a sidebar project** to file it there.
- **Calendar blocks are more solid**, and all-day events sit inset at the top of the day.
- **Notes and links edit in place.** In a task's card, click "Add notes" or "Add link" and start typing. Return starts a new line in notes; click anywhere else to finish.

Fixes

- A task's title is editable on the first click.
- A task card grows to fit its notes instead of cropping its top and bottom.
- The insertion line when reordering a card's tasks is no longer cut off.
