Settings that follow you, and task durations you can tune.

- **Settings sync across your Macs.** Durations, bucket limits, colors, block titles, Notion field mapping, scheduling and calendar display preferences now sync through iCloud. Calendar choices stay per Mac.
- **Default task duration.** Pick 15 minutes to 1½ hours in Settings › Planning. New blocks use it unless the project sets its own.
- **Per-project durations in Notion.** Map a number column on your Projects table in Settings › Fields, or click Create Column, and each project's default duration lives in Notion.
- **Smarter bucket sizing.** Buckets size themselves from their open sub-tasks, capped at a multiple of the project default you choose.
- **New tasks appear instantly.** Press Return and the task slides into the list right away, without waiting for Notion.

Fixes

- Booking a task on a later day no longer moves it into Today.
