Your task order, kept in Notion.

- **Task order lives in Notion.** Map a number column on your Tasks table in Settings › Fields, or click Create Column, and the order you drag tasks into is saved on the tasks themselves. It's the same on every device, and in any Notion view sorted by that column. Your current arrangement is copied over on the next sync.
