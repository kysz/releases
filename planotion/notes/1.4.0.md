Share your availability by email.

- **Scheduling.** Turn on the calendar-clock button in the toolbar, then drag across the calendar to mark when you're free. Drag again to add more times, on any day; click a marked time to unmark it.
- **Copy a snippet.** Copy puts your free times on the clipboard as plain text, grouped by day and ready to paste into an email. Times next to each other show as one range, like 9:00 – 10:00 AM.
- **Pick the meeting length.** Choose 15 minutes to 1½ hours. The snippet names it, and leaves out marked times too short to fit it.
- **Show other time zones.** Tick Time zones and add any you like. Each time then appears in every zone, with the weekday added where it falls on a different day.
